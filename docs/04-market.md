# Market and pricing

Benchmarks gathered 26 Sep 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

| Benchmark | Figure |
|---|---|
| Offshore redline updates | About US$55 per sheet on average (US$155 rush); US$10 to 12 per hour; about 3 hours per sheet |
| UK redline incorporation | £50 to £80 per drawing |
| Australian outsourced drafting | A$45 to 75 per hour |
| Australian Revit drafter salary | About A$75k per year |
| Australian senior engineer charge-out | A$150 to 350+ per hour (structural) |
| Australian engineering consulting businesses | 46,423 (IBISWorld 2026), mostly very small |

## Two cost stories (keep them separate)

- **Small offices:** the markups land on senior engineers. Two days of senior time at A$200 per hour is about A$3,200 per markup set, paid by the owner.
- **Firms with juniors:** the markups land on a junior engineer or drafter. In Connor's case that was a full 16-hour work week in September 2026. Cheaper per hour, but it is the whole of a junior's week.

## The price to beat

A firm could send the same work offshore for a few hundred dollars. But firms that want control of their drawings keep the work in-house, and on secure government projects offshore drafting may not be allowed at all. Why a firm does not outsource (security, turnaround, project context or habit) tells us what the product really competes with. Still to be confirmed with a senior engineer.

## Pricing thoughts

- Per-seat plugin pricing like JustAsk's US$29 per month would be fatal.
- Price per firm against hours saved: per markup set or per sheet (below the outsourcing price, faster, and never leaving the building), or a firm subscription around A$400 to 1,000 per month.
- Paid onboarding: setting up a firm's standard tool set from its family library and template.
